NOIR CAFÉ — the launch film ☕

Thirty seconds, poured slowly.

An espresso falls in slow motion and the steam clears to show the name.
Then the site itself: glass navigation, a hero you scroll through, drink cards whose roast pips fill as you touch them.
The Brewing Lab moves through five transformations (origin, roast, grind, brew, pour), each cut timed to the coffee.
You book a window table, find a quiet corner in one of three New York rooms, and end with good tools for better mornings.

Every frame is the real build: real tokens, real copy, one easing curve.
The sound is made for it: the pour, the grinder, the steam, a cup set down, soft piano.

Coffee is a ritual.
Design should be too.

Sound on 🔊
