# Noir Café — brag plan

**Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 30fps · **Tone:** cinematic · **Duration:** 21.5s

## Answers
- **What is it?** A seven-page website for a New York specialty café, built like a film: espresso footage, scroll-driven chapters, one motion language.
- **Who is it for?** People who judge a studio by its craft — clients, designers, recruiters. It shows what Sams Studio does.
- **What sets it apart?** Motion is the story: the homepage literally makes a coffee as you scroll (Origin → Brew → Extract → Pour → The cup).
- **Best claim:** "Where Every Cup Tells A Story" — the site's own H1.
- **Visual hook:** a macro espresso pour in near-black with warm grain, and a trailer line over it.
- **Real UI to show:** the mobile hero (glass nav, masked headline, buttons), the scroll-story chapters, the Noir Cortado drink card with roast pips, the V60 brew timer.
- **Share caption:** "Some websites load. This one pours."

## Angle
A movie trailer for a coffee website. The trailer voice is the site's own copy; the footage is the site's own films.
