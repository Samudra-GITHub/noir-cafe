NOIR CAFÉ ☕
A cinematic coffee experience.

It started with one question:
what if a website moved at the pace of a pour-over?

Not faster. Not louder.
Just considered.

So every surface was traced from Figma and checked against it, section by section, until the numbers matched.
The hero is a film you scroll through.
The steam is pure CSS.
Five chapters, from seed to cup, unfold as you scroll.
One easing curve moves everything, and all of it steps aside for anyone who prefers reduced motion.

Underneath: tokens, primitives and a single motion language — Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Framer Motion and Lenis — so the seventh page feels as cared for as the first.

Coffee is a ritual.
Design should be too.

Swipe through all ten →
Then tell me: which section would you sit with the longest?

Source is open on GitHub — link in bio.

— Sams Studio
