# Noir Café — press kit

Noir Café is a concept brand and website designed and engineered by **Sams Studio**.
The brand details below are the site's own content (`src/data/story.ts`,
`src/data/locations.ts`); the project facts are measured (`../source/facts.json`).

## Boilerplate

**Short (25 words)**
Noir Café is a cinematic specialty-coffee website by Sams Studio — four languages,
installable, accessible to WCAG 2.2 AA, and built pixel-exact from its Figma design.

**Long**
Noir Café imagines a New York specialty-coffee house that moves at the pace of a
pour-over. Sams Studio designed the identity and built the site in Next.js 16 from
seven Figma frames, then grew it phase by phase — a mobile-first product below
768px, an atmosphere that follows New York's clock and weather, reservations with a
wallet-style pass, order-ahead, a recipe studio, an AI barista grounded in the menu,
and an installable offline app — while the desktop stayed within 0.021% of the
original design. It speaks English, Japanese, French and Italian, prices in USD, EUR,
JPY or GBP, and has zero automated accessibility violations in every language.

## Logos (`logos/`)

| File | Use |
| :-- | :-- |
| `noir-horizontal-espresso` | Primary lockup on light grounds |
| `noir-horizontal-beige` | Primary lockup on dark grounds and photography |
| `noir-stacked-espresso` / `-beige` | Square and portrait formats, with the descriptor |
| `noir-mark-espresso` / `-beige` | The ring alone — avatars, favicons, stamps |

Each is an SVG (wordmark font embedded) and a PNG at 2×. Keep clear space of at least
the ring's radius on every side; don't recolour outside espresso `#17120E`, beige
`#F8F4EC` and caramel `#A86A3C`, stretch, or set the wordmark in another typeface.
